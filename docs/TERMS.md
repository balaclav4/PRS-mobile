**The App is a measurement and record-keeping tool. It is not a substitute for
published load data, and nothing in it is a safety authority.**

The App calculates group statistics, ballistic trajectories, charge-ladder
analysis, pressure work-up trends and seating-depth comparisons from numbers you
enter. Those outputs depend entirely on the accuracy of your inputs and on
conditions the App cannot observe.

You agree that:

- **You will not use the App to determine whether a load is safe to fire.**
  Always work from current published data issued by the powder, bullet, primer
  or firearm manufacturer, and work up from a safe starting charge.
- **The App cannot detect pressure.** Its charge work-up analysis identifies
  patterns in the velocity numbers you enter. A load can be dangerously
  over-pressure while producing velocities the App reports as unremarkable.
- **Ballistic solutions are predictions**, sensitive to muzzle velocity,
  ballistic coefficient, atmospheric conditions and your rifle. Confirm them by
  shooting before relying on them.
