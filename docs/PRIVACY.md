## The short version

Your shooting data stays on your phone. We do not sell it, we do not advertise
against it, and there is no analytics or tracking software in the App.

The only thing we require is an email address, so you can have an account.

Target photographs are **never** uploaded unless you explicitly turn on
Training Data Contribution, which is off by default.

---

## 1. What we collect

### 1.1 Account information

When you create an account we collect your **email address** and store a
password hash through our authentication provider. We never see or store your
password in readable form.

### 1.2 Data you record in the App

Shooting sessions, shot coordinates, rifles, loads, dope cards, load
development projects, round counts and your unit and appearance preferences.

This is stored **on your device**.

**If you use the App with an account,** a copy is also stored in our database
under your account identifier, so that it reaches your other devices and
survives losing this one. It is associated with your account and is not shared
with anyone else.

**If you chose to use the App without an account,** none of it leaves your
device, and we hold nothing about you at all.

### 1.3 Target photographs - only if you opt in

By default, photographs are used on your device to measure a group and are then
discarded. A saved target keeps only the shot coordinates and the reference
corners you marked. **No image leaves your device.**

If you switch on **Training Data Contribution** in Settings, then for sessions
captured while it is on we upload:

- the normalised target photograph;
- the shot positions you marked or confirmed;
- the point of aim you marked;
- the reference corners and the reference dimensions you entered;
- the caliber, where you have recorded it.

We use this to train and evaluate the bullet-hole detection models in the App.

**What is not included:** your name, email, account identifier, rifle names,
load names, session names, notes, or any other data from your account. The
upload is not linked to your account in the training set.

**Camera metadata is removed.** The App re-encodes every photograph before it is
used, which strips EXIF data - including any camera model, timestamp or GPS
coordinates the original file contained. This happens regardless of whether you
contribute images, because the App requires an upright, metadata-free image to
measure accurately.

### 1.4 What we never collect

- **Location.** The App contains no location code and requests no location
  permission.
- **Analytics or behavioural tracking.** There is no analytics, advertising or
  session-recording software in the App.
- **Contacts, calendar, microphone, or health data.**
- **Payment information.** [Delete this line if you add purchases.]

### 1.5 Technical data

Our authentication and database provider records standard operational
information such as request timestamps and IP addresses for security and abuse
prevention. We do not use this to build a profile of you.

## 3. Training Data Contribution in detail

**It is off unless you turn it on.** We will not enable it for you, and we will
not ask again through a nagging prompt.

**You can turn it off at any time** in Settings. Turning it off stops any
further uploads immediately.

**Withdrawing consent.** Contact us at [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL] and we will
delete images you have contributed, provided they remain identifiable to your
submission. Because contributed images are stored without account identifiers,
we may need you to tell us roughly when you contributed in order to locate them.

> **Please be aware:** once an image has been used to train a model, that
> training cannot be reversed. We will delete the image; we cannot un-learn it
> from a model that has already been trained. Do not contribute an image you are
> not comfortable being used this way.

**Check the photograph before contributing.** Photographs are of paper targets,
but you are the only person who can see what else is in frame. Do not contribute
an image containing faces, vehicle number plates, addresses, documents, or
anything identifying a location.
